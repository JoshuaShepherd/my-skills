# Before and after

Worked pairs, one per failure mode.

**Register (costume → plain)**
- Before: "We leverage the relational substrate to surface credibility at scale."
- After: "We use the relationships you already have to make your credibility visible, widely."

**Hype → relief**
- Before: "Unlock the transformative power of AI for your mission!"
- After: "You do not have to get this wrong. Here is the first step, and it is free."

**Performing → plain**
- Before: "Our platform operationalizes a robust, end-to-end content architecture."
- After: "We help you publish your work well, and keep it findable."

**Em dash → period**
- Before: "The work is real and it's working — but internally, you're fragmented."
- After: "The work is real, and it is working. Internally, you are fragmented."

**No concession → concession**
- Before: "Movemental is better than a website builder."
- After: "A website builder makes a fine site, and it is cheaper. It just cannot do the part that matters most for your work."
