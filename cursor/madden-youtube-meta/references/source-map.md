# Madden competitive strategy sources (YouTube-first)

Use this as a **starting map**, not gospel. Competitive Madden meta shifts with patches, tournaments (MCS), and rule sets (Salary Cap vs MUT vs regs). Re-verify **game year** and **mode** before treating any play as “current.”

## Sources that tend to show *extractable* scheme detail

These creators or brands usually verbalize **playbook**, **formation**, **play name**, **motion**, **hot routes**, and sometimes **coverage** — enough to rebuild the concept in your notes.

| Source | Why it is extractable | Caveat |
|--------|------------------------|--------|
| **Civil / MisterCiv / The Madden Academy ecosystem** | Long-form breakdowns of team playbooks; often names formations and plays; ties to `civil.gg` / `themaddenacademy.com` for deeper schemes | Some funnel to paid memberships; brand has multiple channels (e.g., Civil Premium vs academy-style tutorials). |
| **ZAN** | Very high tutorial volume; defensive shell and offensive concept videos; links out to `gridirongameplans.gg` for structured game plans | Huge library — prefer **guide-style** uploads over short clips when mining plays. |
| **Huddle.gg (YouTube: HuddleGG)** | Pros teaching route combos, shells, and gameplans; names competitive staples (e.g., Trips TE families, 3-high looks) | Strong paid tier; YouTube is often a **slice** of full gameplans. |
| **MaddenTurf** (site + occasional video) | Belt-level pros; written guides often more complete than any single video | **Not YouTube-primary**; use as cross-check when a video references “vault” or “tip vault.” |
| **Madden Prodigy / similar ebook vendors** | Stepwise schemes by formation | Paid PDF/ebook; copyright — extract **ideas** into your own notes, do not redistribute vendor text. |

## Five strong “teacher-first” picks (strategy, not “watch me farm wins”)

These are chosen for **instructional intent** and **repeatable systems**. None are perfect; skim thumbnails for “pack opening” vs “full guide.”

1. **Civil + The Madden Academy** — Playbook-centric teaching, coaching voice, competitive background; best when you need **named plays** and **audible logic**.
2. **ZAN** — Systematic tutorials across offense/defense; good for **coverage rules** and **adjustment heuristics**; pair site resources when cited.
3. **HuddleGG** — Closest thing on YouTube to **pro-authored gameplans** broken into lessons; use when you want **route combinations** and **defensive shells** pros actually ran in tournaments.
4. **Gutfoxx** — Strong on **why the meta is the meta** (balance, Salary Cap, patch impacts). Use for **meta literacy**, not always full playbook callouts in every video.
5. **Eric Rayweather** — Mixed catalog (news, MUT, tips). Use **selectively**: search his channel for “tips,” “guide,” “best defense,” “full breakdown,” and skip economy/pack content unless that is your mode.

**Honest exclusions**

- **Throne** and similar large creators: valuable, but historically a blend of entertainment, MUT, and gameplay volume — filter aggressively for tutorial series.
- **Gutfoxx** alone is not enough if your goal is a **playbook sheet**; pair with 1–3 above.

## Non-YouTube checks (recommended)

- **Operation Sports** forums — patch talk, community labbing.
- **EA competitive / MCS** — what rule set pros actually play under.

## Legal / ethics

- Transcripts for **personal research** are fine; **do not** republish paid ebook text or members-only PDFs.
- Attribute ideas to **channel + video title + approximate date** in your notes.
